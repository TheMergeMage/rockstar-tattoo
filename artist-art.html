Art's Portfolio

Known for: custom tattooing shaped around each client's unique vision.

Hi I'm Arturo Ramirez, but you can call me Art. I believe a great tattoo should be as unique as the person wearing it. With over 20 years of tattooing experience, I take pride in creating custom pieces that combine creativity, precision, and lasting quality, whether you’re bringing a fully developed idea or just the start of a vision. My goal is to make every client feel comfortable, heard, and excited throughout the process while delivering artwork they’ll be proud to wear for a lifetime. Take a look through my portfolio, and when you’re ready to bring your next tattoo to life, ask for Art.

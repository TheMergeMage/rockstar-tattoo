Artist Profile

Sheylla's Portfolio

Known for: black & grey, fine line, and small-to-medium custom pieces.

Sheylla, also known as Shey Legon, has been tattooing for over one year and has been dedicated to growing as a tattoo artist, focusing on black & grey, fine line, and now learning more about microrealism tattoos. She especially enjoys creating small and medium-sized custom pieces that feel personal and timeless. Every tattoo is approached with care, attention to detail, and a commitment to making sure each client leaves with something they’ll be proud to wear for a lifetime. She is constantly learning, refining her craft, and dedicated to giving every person who sits in the chair the best experience possible. 🤍🌸

  • Black & Grey

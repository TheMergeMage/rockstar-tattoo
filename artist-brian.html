Artist Profile

Brian's Portfolio

Known for: traditional, neo-traditional, full-color tattooing, and professional piercing experience.

I am a professional tattoo artist with 24 years of experience in the tattoo industry and over 15 years of professional piercing experience. Throughout my career, I have built a strong reputation for quality work, professionalism, and creating custom pieces that stand out through bold color and solid design.

For 11 years, I owned and operated Mastermind Tattoo in Las Vegas, where I developed a loyal client base and gained extensive experience managing both the artistic and business sides of the industry. Owning my own shop helped strengthen my customer service skills, work ethic, and ability to create a welcoming environment for clients from all backgrounds.

My strongest styles are traditional, neo-traditional, and full-color tattooing. I especially enjoy creating vibrant floral work and any design that allows bold color saturation and creativity to stand out. Full-color tattoos are where I feel most confident and passionate as an artist.

After more than two decades in the industry, I continue to stay motivated by artistic growth, client relationships, and delivering high-quality tattoos that clients are proud to wear for life. I bring reliability, experience, versatility, and a strong passion for tattooing to every shop I work with.

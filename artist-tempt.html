Artist Profile

Tempt's Portfolio

Known for: custom tattoo design, large-scale work, color pieces, and black and grey.

My name is Richard Tran, but most people in the tattoo industry know me as Tempt. I have been tattooing professionally for over 30 years, dedicating my life to the craft, culture, and evolution of tattooing. Throughout my career, I have worked hard to develop a strong reputation for creativity, consistency, and professionalism while building lasting relationships with clients from all walks of life.

Tattooing has always been more than a job to me. It is an art form that allows me to turn ideas, memories, and emotions into permanent pieces people carry with pride. Over the decades, I have continued to grow as an artist by adapting to new styles, techniques, and trends while staying true to the fundamentals that built my foundation.

My strengths include custom tattoo design, large-scale work, color pieces, black and grey, and creating artwork tailored specifically to each client. I take pride in maintaining a clean, safe, and welcoming environment while delivering high-quality work and strong customer experiences.

Throughout my career, I have had the opportunity to work alongside talented artists, build loyal clientele, and contribute to the tattoo community through dedication and passion for the craft. My goal has always been to push myself creatively while giving clients artwork they can be proud of for a lifetime.

After more than 30 years in the industry, I still carry the same passion and hunger for tattooing that I had when I first started. I look forward to continuing my journey, creating meaningful art, and bringing experience, professionalism, and creativity wherever I work.
